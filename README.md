# Smart Center

Small utilities for your Mac, in one place.

**[Visit the website](https://alexanderbeef.github.io/smart-center-releases/)** · **[Download for Mac](https://github.com/AlexanderBeef/smart-center-releases/releases/latest/download/Smart-Center.dmg)**

Smart Center lives in the menu bar and brings together a set of small tools, called modules.
Turn on the ones you need, and use them from its settings, the menu bar, or Control Center.

## Modules

**Always on**
- **Display Corners** — Round the corners of the displays you choose, in every Space.
- **Window Tiling** — Drag a window to an edge or corner of the screen to tile it.
- **Quit on Close** — Apps quit when you close their last window, except the ones you choose. You can also require holding ⌘Q to quit.
- **Minimize from Dock** — Click the Dock icon of the app you're using to minimize its windows.
- **Fill Screen on Click** — The green button and a double-click on the title bar fill the screen, leaving a margin.
- **Menu Bar Management** — Hide the app icons you don't need, and show them again with the pointer or from a menu.

**Tools**
- **Color Picker** — Pick a color anywhere on your screen and copy its code.
- **Keyboard Cleaning** — Lock all keys while you clean your keyboard.
- **Keep Awake** — Keep your Mac awake on demand, on a schedule, or while an app is open, with a battery limit.
- **Scratchpad** — A floating pad for quick notes, saved automatically.
- **Camera Preview** — A floating mirror to check how you look before a call.
- **Text Capture** — Copy the text in an area of the screen.
- **Clipboard History** — Keep the text you copy and paste it again later, or paste as plain text with ⌥⇧⌘V.

**Coming soon:** Display Control, Window Switcher, Dock Preview, Dynamic Notch, Drag Shelf, File Converter, Keyboard Shortcuts.

## Download

Download the latest version from the [website](https://alexanderbeef.github.io/smart-center-releases/) or from [Releases](https://github.com/AlexanderBeef/smart-center-releases/releases/latest).
It's signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings.

Open the downloaded file and drag Smart Center to the Applications folder, then open it from there.
New versions are installed from the app.

## Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later. Tested on macOS 27.

## Privacy

Smart Center doesn't collect, send, or share any information. It connects to the internet only to
check for updates. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
