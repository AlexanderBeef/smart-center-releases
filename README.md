# Smart Center

Small utilities for your Mac, in one place.

Smart Center lives in the menu bar and brings together a set of small tools, called modules.
Turn on the ones you need, and use them from its settings, the menu bar, or Control Center.

## Modules

**Always on**
- **Display Corners** — Round the corners of the displays you choose, in every Space.
- **Window Tiling** — Drag a window to an edge or corner of the screen to tile it.
- **Quit on Close** — Apps quit when you close their last window, except the ones you choose.
- **Minimize from Dock** — Click the Dock icon of the app you're using to minimize its windows.
- **Fill Screen on Click** — The green button and a double-click on the title bar fill the screen, leaving a margin.
- **Menu Bar Management** — Hide the app icons you don't need, and show them again with the pointer or from a menu.

**Tools**
- **Color Picker** — Pick a color anywhere on your screen and copy its code.
- **Keyboard Cleaning** — Lock all keys while you clean your keyboard.
- **Keep Awake** — Keep your Mac awake on demand, on a schedule, or while an app is open.
- **Scratchpad** — A floating pad for quick notes, saved automatically.
- **Camera Preview** — A floating mirror to check how you look before a call.
- **Text Capture** — Copy the text in an area of the screen.
- **Clipboard History** — Keep the text you copy and paste it again later.

**Coming soon:** Dynamic Notch, File Converter, Keyboard Shortcuts.
