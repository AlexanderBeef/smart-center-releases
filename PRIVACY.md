# Privacy

Smart Center doesn't collect, send, or share any information about you or your Mac.

- **No accounts, no analytics.** Smart Center has no sign-in and doesn't measure how you use it.
- **Your data stays on your Mac.** What modules save — your notes in Scratchpad, the text kept by
  Clipboard History, your settings — is stored only on this Mac.
- **Clipboard History** keeps the text you copy, on this Mac only. Items that apps mark as private,
  such as passwords from a password manager, are skipped.
- **Screen and camera.** Nothing on your screen or from the camera is recorded. Menu Bar Management
  keeps only pictures of the menu bar icons, on this Mac. Camera Preview shows the camera only in
  its own window.
- **Permissions.** Each module asks only for the macOS permissions it needs, and you choose whether
  to allow them. You can change them at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- **Updates.** Smart Center connects to the internet only to check for updates: it reads the list of
  versions from this page on GitHub and, when you choose to install one, downloads it from here.
  No personal information is sent. You can turn off automatic checks in Smart Center › About.
